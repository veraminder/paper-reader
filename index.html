            Where are my notes stored?

            Everything you save — notes, folders, and the PDFs they belong to — is kept inside this browser, on this device. It is not uploaded anywhere: not to GitHub, not to a server, and nobody else can see it. Your name and Gemini key are kept the same way.

            Each device and browser has its own library

            • iPad and iPhone: add Paper Reader to your Home Screen (in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen) and always open it from that icon. The Home Screen app keeps its own library, separate from Safari tabs. If you use it in a normal Safari tab instead, Safari deletes its data after 7 days in which you haven’t opened it.
            • Mac with Safari: the same 7-day rule applies to normal tabs. On macOS 14 or newer you can avoid it with File → Add to Dock and opening it from the Dock.
            • Chrome, Edge or Firefox (Windows, Mac, Android, Chromebook): kept until you clear the browser’s data.
            • Libraries don’t sync: notes made on your iPad aren’t on your laptop. Use Back up and Restore to move them.

            How notes can be lost

            • Clearing browsing data — e.g. iPad Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data, or Chrome’s Delete browsing data with “Cookies and site data” ticked.
            • Deleting the Paper Reader icon from the Home Screen.
            • Using a private or incognito window: everything is gone when it closes.
            • A nearly full device: browsers may clear website data to free space. Paper Reader asks the browser to keep its data, but that isn’t a guarantee.
            • The 7-day rule in Safari tabs (see above).

            Back up and Restore

            • Back up saves everything — notes, folders and the PDFs — as one file. Keep it somewhere safe, like iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Files, and make a new one every so often.
            • Restore loads a backup file. It only adds: nothing already in your library is deleted. If the same paper is in both, the more recently saved version is kept. Use it to move your library to a new device or browser.
            • Your name and Gemini key aren’t in backup files.
